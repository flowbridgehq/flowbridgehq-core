# FlowBridgeHQ
Decentralized bridge for ISO 20022-compliant cryptocurrencies like XRP, XLM, HBAR, ALGO, and XDC, powered by the Interledger Protocol (ILP).

## Vision
Unite blockchain finance for seamless cross-border payments and DeFi.

## Roadmap
- **6–12 Months (Late 2025–Mid 2026)**: Launch MVP bridging XRP and XLM with ILP connectors.
- **2–3 Years (2027–2028)**: Integrate HBAR, ALGO, XDC, and secure bank/DeFi partnerships.
- **5 Years (2030)**: Support all ISO 20022-compliant coins and major blockchains (Ethereum, Solana) for global finance.

## Status
Early development. Building in private, going public soon. Follow us on X: [@FlowBridgeHQ](https://x.com/FlowBridgeHQ).
